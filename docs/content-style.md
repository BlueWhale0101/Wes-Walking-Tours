# Content Style

The guide should feel like a serious adult field companion: practical enough to use outside, rich enough to make the walk better than signage alone.

Use `docs/script-production.md` for the full research, drafting, and review process for stop scripts. This page is the short style reference.

## Preferred Voice

- Specific, grounded, and curious.
- More museum field lecture than tourist brochure.
- Military, history, geology, ecology, and infrastructure details are welcome.
- Explain constraints: heat, materials, distance, water, labor, transport, maintenance.

## Script Checklist

Before a script is done, check:

- Does it tell the listener what to notice?
- Does it include dates, numbers, names, or mechanisms?
- Does it explain why this place is here, not just what it is?
- Does it identify a central pressure: conflict, constraint, tradeoff, risk, failure, decision, or adaptation?
- Does it include documented human pressure, not just generic user groups?
- Does it avoid generic travel copy?
- Does each paragraph end cleanly for TTS?
- Does it work without opening a link?

## Stop Script Standard

Each full stop should reveal a larger system under pressure through visible evidence. The listener should be able to look up from the audio and see why the script is talking about law, land, ecology, water, labour, transport, engineering, tourism, public health, Indigenous history, regional administration, memory, maintenance, or economic change.

Every paragraph should include concrete facts before interpretation. Use dates, names, materials, institutions, costs, functions, conflicts, design choices, environmental constraints, and documented human use.

Avoid generic interpretive signposts such as "this matters," "the contrast is the point," "this reflects change," "the timing is important," "this place tells a story," and "this is more than just." Replace them with evidence.

## TTS Notes

Generate audio as local MP3 files and keep them in the guide folder. OpenAI speech models can be tested as an alternative to ElevenLabs when paragraph endings and narration naturalness matter.
